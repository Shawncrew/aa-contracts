How this is built

Read live from corptools' synced contract data — contracts assigned to {{ alliance_ids|join:", " }} that are still outstanding and not expired. corptools still lists {{ totals.stale_outstanding|intcomma }} as "outstanding", but most of those already expired in-game and haven't re-synced, so only unexpired, unaccepted contracts are counted here ({{ totals.live_total|intcomma }}). Hull is read from synced item rows where present ({{ totals.with_item_data|intcomma }} contracts), otherwise from the contract title. {{ totals.unidentified|intcomma }} contracts could not be matched to a ship and are grouped as “Unidentified”. The raw figures are also available as JSON.
