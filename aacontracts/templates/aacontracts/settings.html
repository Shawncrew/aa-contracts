How each item is priced at Jita 4-4 (Fuzzwork):
  • Sell — percentile: a robust “lowest sell” that ignores a handful of outlier-cheap orders. Closest to what you actually pay to buy. Recommended.
  • Sell — lowest order: the single cheapest sell order (can be a bait/outlier price).
  • Buy — percentile: a robust “highest buy” — what buyers are currently paying.
  • Buy — highest order: the single highest buy order.
  • Split: the average of the lowest sell and the highest buy — a middle “fair” value between buying and selling.
